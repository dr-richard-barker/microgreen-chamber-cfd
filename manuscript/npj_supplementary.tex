\section*{Supplementary Note 1: Mesh Convergence and Spatial Independence}

Spatial discretization error was quantified using a three-level systematic refinement ladder ($m0$, $m1$, $m2$) spanning background cell base sizes of $6.67\text{ mm}$ ($m0$), $3.33\text{ mm}$ ($m1$), and $1.67\text{ mm}$ ($m2$). SnappyHexMesh surface and volume refinement criteria were held constant across levels.

As detailed in Table~\ref{tab:mesh_convergence}, volume-averaged canopy velocity ($\overline{U}_{canopy}$) converged within $<1.3\%$ between the intermediate $m1$ and fine $m2$ grids across all three hardware geometries, while reducing computational runtime by $\sim8\times$. Consequently, the $m1$ level ($\sim1.07\text{M} - 4.85\text{M}$ cells) was selected as the optimal production baseline for all multi-gravity sweeps.

\section*{Supplementary Note 2: OpenFOAM Numerical Discretization and Solver Settings}

All simulations utilized second-order spatial discretization schemes:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Gradient terms: \texttt{cellLimited Gauss linear 1.0}
    \item Convective divergence terms for momentum ($\nabla \cdot (\rho \mathbf{u}\mathbf{u})$): \texttt{bounded Gauss linearUpwind grad(U)}
    \item Turbulent kinetic energy and dissipation rate ($\nabla \cdot (\rho \mathbf{u} k)$, $\nabla \cdot (\rho \mathbf{u} \omega)$): \texttt{bounded Gauss limitedLinear 0.33}
    \item Enthalpy divergence ($\nabla \cdot (\rho \mathbf{u} h)$): \texttt{bounded Gauss linearUpwind grad(h)}
    \item Laplacian terms: \texttt{Gauss linear limited corrected 0.33}
\end{itemize}

Pressure-velocity coupling for steady state utilized the SIMPLE algorithm with relaxation factors of $0.3$ for pressure and $0.7$ for momentum/turbulence. Transient simulations used PIMPLE with 3 outer correctors and adaptive time-stepping bound to a maximum Courant number $Co_{max} \le 6.0$ ($Co_{jet} \le 2.6$).
